Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how simonlinuxia.github.io/mes-niches-site/ ("the Site") handles information. This is a personal, editorial website publishing practical guides in French, English, and Spanish. We take a minimal-data approach: we do not require account creation, and we do not operate any comment system, newsletter signup, or user login.

Information we collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or physical address through the Site. The Site is a static website with no server-side database of visitors and no user accounts.

Aggregate analytics

We use Google Search Console to understand, in aggregate, which pages are indexed and how the Site performs in Google Search results (e.g. impressions, clicks, and approximate ranking position for search queries). This data is provided by Google in aggregated, non-identifying form and is not used to track individual visitors.

Hosting

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc.). Like most web hosts, GitHub Pages may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operational purposes. This logging is performed by GitHub, not by us directly. You can review GitHub's own privacy practices at docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

Third-party links and social media

The Site may link to third-party websites and services, including Amazon and our Pinterest profile. These third parties operate independently and have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set tracking cookies. Third-party services linked from the Site (such as Amazon or Pinterest) may set their own cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies once you visit or interact with those services.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
