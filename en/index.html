01 / FOUNDATION

Core Operating Values

The principles behind every guide we publish.

Practicality

Every piece of advice is meant to be usable with everyday resources — if it can't be done realistically, it doesn't get published.

Honesty

Direct, factual answers without unnecessary padding or filler explanations — just what works and what doesn't.

Simplicity

Clear formatting and straightforward language, designed to deliver an answer when you actually need it.
