Quick and Budget-Friendly Meals for Students

Published on October 1, 2026

As a student, balancing academics and a tight budget can be challenging, but it’s entirely possible to prepare quick and economical meals that are both healthy and satisfying. Here are five budget-friendly meal ideas that will help you stay on track without breaking the bank.

Photo: Alexy Almond / Pexels

1. Omelette with Spinach and Mushrooms

Ingredients:

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup of fresh spinach leaves
  • 3-4 medium-sized mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon of olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation:

  1. Cook the Mushrooms: Heat the olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the sliced mushrooms and sauté for 3-4 minutes until they are soft and golden.
  2. Add the Spinach: Add the spinach to the pan and cook for another 2 minutes until it wilts. Remove from heat.
  3. Beat the Eggs: In a separate bowl, beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Cook the Omelette: Pour the beaten eggs into the pan with the mushrooms and spinach. Let them cook for 1-2 minutes, then use a spatula to fold the omelette.
  5. Serve: Cook for an additional 1-2 minutes until the eggs are set. Serve immediately.

Cost: Approximately $1.50

2. Lentil Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of dried lentils
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • 1 cup of vegetable broth
  • 1 can of diced tomatoes
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of olive oil

Preparation:

  1. Soak the Lentils: Rinse the lentils and soak them in water for 1-2 hours.
  2. Sauté the Vegetables: Heat the olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the onion and sauté for 3-4 minutes until translucent.
  3. Add Other Ingredients: Add the garlic, carrot, and celery. Sauté for 2-3 more minutes.
  4. Cook the Lentils: Add the soaked lentils, vegetable broth, and diced tomatoes. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 25-30 minutes until the lentils are soft.
  5. Season and Serve: Season with salt and pepper. Serve hot.

Cost: Approximately $2.00

3. Chicken and Rice Stir-Fry

Ingredients:

  • 1 chicken breast, sliced into thin strips
  • 1 cup of long-grain rice
  • 1 cup of frozen mixed vegetables
  • 2 tablespoons of soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil
  • Salt to taste

Preparation:

  1. Cook the Rice: Rinse the rice and cook it according to package instructions. Set aside.
  2. Stir-Fry the Chicken: Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and stir-fry for 4-5 minutes until it is browned and cooked through.
  3. Add the Vegetables: Add the frozen mixed vegetables and cook for 2-3 minutes until heated through.
  4. Combine and Season: Add the cooked rice and soy sauce. Stir to combine and heat for another 2 minutes. Season with salt if needed.
  5. Serve: Serve the chicken and rice stir-fry hot.

Cost: Approximately $2.50

4. Tuna Salad with Whole Wheat Crackers

Ingredients:

  • 1 can of tuna in water, drained
  • 1 small celery stalk, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 10 whole wheat crackers

Preparation:

  1. Prepare the Tuna Salad: In a bowl, mix the drained tuna with the chopped celery and mayonnaise. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Serve: Spoon the tuna salad onto the whole wheat crackers and enjoy.

Cost: Approximately $1.00

5. Quick Smoothie Bowl

Ingredients:

  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1/2 cup of frozen berries
  • 1/2 cup of plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon of honey
  • Toppings: 2 tablespoons of granola, 1 tablespoon of sliced almonds

Preparation:

  1. Blend the Smoothie: In a blender, combine the frozen banana, frozen berries, Greek yogurt, and honey. Blend until smooth.
  2. Serve: Pour the smoothie into a bowl and top with granola and sliced almonds. Enjoy immediately.

Cost: Approximately $1.00

These five budget-friendly meals offer a variety of flavors and textures, ensuring that you don’t get bored while keeping your expenses in check. Whether you’re in a rush or just looking for a healthy, economical meal, these options are perfect for any student on a tight budget.
