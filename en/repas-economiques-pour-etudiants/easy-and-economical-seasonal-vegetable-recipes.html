Easy and Economical Seasonal Vegetable Recipes

Published on September 19, 2026

Seasonal vegetables are tastier and usually cheaper. Here is how to choose, store and cook them, with three simple recipes that serve four.

Choose and store

In autumn and winter you find carrots, leeks, turnips, squash, cabbage and potatoes. In summer, zucchini, tomatoes and eggplant are plentiful. Store root vegetables in a cool, dark place and delicate vegetables in the refrigerator. Wash them before cooking.

1. Leek and carrot soup

Ingredients: 3 leeks, 2 carrots, 1 onion, 2 potatoes, 1 liter of vegetable broth, 1 tablespoon of oil, salt and pepper. Soften the onion, add the other chopped vegetables, cover with broth and simmer for 25 minutes. Blend if you like.

2. Carrot quiche

Ingredients: 1 pie crust, 3 grated carrots, 3 eggs, 200 ml of milk, salt and pepper. Sauté the carrots for a few minutes, beat the eggs with the milk, pour everything over the crust and bake at 180 °C (350 °F) for 30 to 35 minutes, until the center is fully set.

3. Tomato and parsley salad

Ingredients: 4 tomatoes, a small bunch of parsley, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, 1 teaspoon of vinegar, salt and pepper. Cut the tomatoes into wedges, chop the parsley and season. Serve fresh.

Reduce waste

  • Use slightly soft vegetables in soup or an omelet, but throw away any that are moldy or smell bad.
  • Freeze vegetables you will not eat in time.
  • Keep cooked leftovers in the refrigerator and eat them within two to three days.

Planning meals around what is in season is one of the simplest ways to eat healthily without spending much.
