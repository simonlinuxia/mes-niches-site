Budget-Friendly Recipes Using Sales and Discounts

Published on October 12, 2026

Photo: Anna Shvets / Pexels

Weekly flyers and store discounts can cut your grocery bill, but only if you plan meals around them. Here is a simple way to do it, with three cheap recipes that work with almost any sale.

How to shop the sales

  • Check the flyer before you write your list, not after.
  • Buy what you will really eat. A discount on food you throw away is not a saving.
  • Compare the price per kilogram or per liter, not the package price.
  • Freeze extra meat, bread and cooked meals the day you buy or cook them.

Recipe 1: Chicken and vegetable rice

Cut chicken thighs or breasts into small pieces and cook them in a little oil until no pink remains and the inside reaches 74 °C (165 °F). Add frozen mixed vegetables and a splash of soy sauce, and serve over rice. Chicken on sale is easy to portion and freeze raw.

Recipe 2: Bean and tomato chili

Soften a chopped onion in a pot, add canned tomatoes, rinsed canned beans, chili powder and cumin, and simmer for 20 minutes. If you add ground beef, brown it first until it reaches 71 °C (160 °F) and drain the fat. Beans keep the cost low.

Recipe 3: Pasta with vegetable sauce

Cook pasta according to the package. Make a quick sauce with canned tomatoes, garlic, onion and any vegetable on sale, such as zucchini or peppers. Finish with a little grated cheese.

Food safety

  • Refrigerate leftovers within two hours and eat them within two to three days.
  • Reheat leftovers until steaming hot, to 74 °C (165 °F).
  • Thaw frozen food in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

Build the week around two or three sale items, then fill in with pantry staples like rice, pasta and beans.
