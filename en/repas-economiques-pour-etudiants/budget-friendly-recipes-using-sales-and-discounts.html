Budget-Friendly Recipes Using Sales and Discounts

Published on October 12, 2026

When you're on a tight budget, every penny counts. However, taking advantage of sales and discounts can help you save big on your grocery bill. This article will guide you through five budget-friendly recipes that you can whip up using the savings from your local store’s sales and discounts. Each recipe is designed to be both delicious and affordable, allowing you to stretch your food dollars further.

Photo: Anna Shvets / Pexels

1. Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, carrots)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Cooked rice for serving

Steps:

  1. Prep the Chicken and Vegetables: Cut the chicken breasts into bite-sized pieces and slice the vegetables. This step can be done the night before to save time.
  2. Sales Hunt: Look for sales on chicken breasts and mixed vegetables. For instance, if chicken breasts are on sale for $1.50 per pound and vegetables are 50 cents per pound, it’s a great time to stock up.
  3. Cooking Process: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until fully browned, about 5 minutes. Remove the chicken from the skillet and set aside.
  4. Add Vegetables: In the same skillet, add the vegetables and cook until they are tender-crisp, about 5 minutes. Return the chicken to the skillet, add soy sauce, and cook for an additional 2 minutes.
  5. Serve: Serve the stir-fry over cooked rice. Enjoy this healthy and tasty meal!

2. Beef and Bean Chili

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 can (15 ounces) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (15 ounces) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup beef broth

Steps:

  1. Check for Sales: Find a sale on ground beef, as it’s often one of the best deals in the meat section. Also, look for discounts on canned beans, which are usually on sale.
  2. Cooking Process: Brown the ground beef in a large pot over medium heat, about 7 minutes. Drain the excess fat.
  3. Add Vegetables: Add the diced onion and red bell pepper to the pot and cook until the vegetables are softened, about 5 minutes.
  4. Seasoning: Stir in the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Add the beans and beef broth, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Serve: Serve the chili with a side of cornbread or rice. This dish is hearty and comforting, perfect for a cold evening.

3. Spaghetti with Meat Sauce

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 1 pound ground turkey or beef
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Parmesan cheese for topping

Steps:

  1. Sales Check: Look for discounts on ground turkey or beef, as well as canned tomatoes. These ingredients often go on sale in bulk, making them a cost-effective choice.
  2. Cooking Process: Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions. In a large pot, heat some olive oil and sauté the chopped onion and minced garlic for 2 minutes. Add the ground meat and cook until browned, about 7 minutes.
  3. Add Sauce: Stir in the crushed tomatoes, dried oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer the sauce for 20 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
  4. Combine: Drain the spaghetti and add it to the pot with the meat sauce. Toss to combine and serve with grated Parmesan cheese.

4. Lentil Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup dried green or brown lentils
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Sales Search: Check for discounts on lentils and vegetable broth. Lentils are often on sale, making them a great option for a protein-packed, budget-friendly meal.
  2. Cooking Process: Rinse the lentils and set aside. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and sauté the onion and garlic for 2 minutes. Add the diced carrot and celery and cook for an additional 5 minutes.
  3. Add Lentils and Broth: Stir in the lentils, vegetable broth, and thyme. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for 30 minutes, or until the lentils are tender.
  4. Serve: Season with salt and pepper to taste. This soup is both nutritious and filling, perfect for a weeknight meal.

5. Quick and Easy Chicken Tacos

Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 4 soft taco shells
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Sales Hunt: Find a sale on chicken breasts and pick up a bag of taco shells that are on discount. This combination will save you a lot of money.
  2. Cooking Process: Cut the chicken breasts into strips and season with salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for about 5 minutes, or until fully cooked. Remove from the skillet and shred with two forks.
  3. Assemble Tacos: Warm the taco shells in a dry skillet for about 30 seconds on each side. Assemble the tacos by filling each shell with shredded chicken, shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, and cheddar cheese.
  4. Serve: Enjoy these quick and delicious tacos. They are perfect for a meal prep day, as you can make them in bulk and freeze them for later.

By following these budget-friendly recipes, you can make delicious meals even when you’re on a tight budget. Always keep an eye out for sales and discounts, and use them to your advantage. Happy cooking!
