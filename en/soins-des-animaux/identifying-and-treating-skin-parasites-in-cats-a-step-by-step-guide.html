Skin Parasites in Cats: Signs, Vet Treatment and Home Clean-Up

Published on October 1, 2026

Photo: Helena Jankovičová Kováčová / Pexels

Cats with itchy skin may have fleas, mites, ticks or a skin infection that looks similar. The most important rule is that cat treatment must come from a vet. Many products for dogs are toxic to cats, and a wrong choice can be fatal.

Common skin parasites

  • fleas: tiny dark insects that jump, causing itching and hair loss;
  • ear mites: dark crumbly debris in the ears and a lot of head shaking;
  • ticks: small bumps attached to the skin, found mostly on cats that go outdoors;
  • other mites and ringworm (a fungus): patches of hair loss and flaky or crusty skin.

Signs to watch for

Scratching, biting at the fur, overgrooming, bald patches, scabs, red skin and black specks in the coat. To check for flea dirt, comb your cat over a white paper towel: the specks that turn reddish when dampened are flea droppings.

Step 1: See your vet

Different parasites need different treatments, and some skin problems look alike. Your vet will examine your cat, may take a sample of skin or ear debris, and will recommend a treatment based on your cat's age and weight.

Step 2: Use only what your vet recommends

  • Never use dog flea or tick products on a cat. Some contain ingredients that are highly toxic to cats.
  • Follow the label and the vet's instructions exactly, and do not combine products.
  • Kittens, pregnant cats and sick cats need special care: ask first.
  • Avoid home remedies, essential oils and homemade treatments, many of which are harmful to cats.

Step 3: Clean the home

Fleas live in carpets and bedding, not only on the cat. Wash bedding in hot water, vacuum floors and furniture often and empty the vacuum bag outside. Other pets in the house will need treatment too, so ask your vet about them.

Step 4: Prevent it from coming back

Regular preventive treatment prescribed by your vet is much more effective than treating an infestation. It matters even for indoor cats, because fleas can come in on people and other pets.

When to go to the vet urgently

Go quickly if a kitten has fleas and seems weak or has pale gums, if the cat has open wounds, if it stops eating or if it is very lethargic.
