Transform Your Cluttered Library Into a Practical Storage Space: 5 Steps to Success

Published on October 5, 2026

Photo: Alexas_Fotos / Pixabay

A cluttered bookshelf can become tidy storage in an afternoon. Here are five steps that work, with safety tips for tall shelves.

Step 1: Empty and sort

Take everything off the shelves. Sort into keep, donate or sell, and recycle. Be honest about what you will actually read or use again.

Step 2: Clean the shelves

Dust and wipe each shelf with a slightly damp cloth, then let them dry before putting things back.

Step 3: Group and place

Group items by type: books, papers, decor, supplies. Put the heaviest items on the lower shelves and lighter ones higher. Keep what you use often between waist and shoulder height.

Step 4: Add organizers

Bins, baskets, dividers and bookends turn open shelves into tidy storage. Label each container. Use baskets with lids for small objects and cables.

Step 5: Secure the shelf

A tall bookcase can tip over, especially with children or pets around. Fasten it to a wall stud with an anti-tip strap or bracket supplied with the shelf or sold at hardware stores. Do not overload shelves: respect the maximum load of each one.

Other safety points

  • Do not climb on shelves.
  • Use a step stool for high shelves.
  • Keep the area in front of the shelves clear.

Keep it organized

Once a month, take a few minutes to put things back and remove what you no longer need. For every new item, let one go.

With a little sorting and a few organizers, a cluttered bookcase becomes useful, safe storage.
